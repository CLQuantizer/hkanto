## Design Context

### Users

People translating English into natural Hong Kong Cantonese for everyday conversation. They need two useful, immediately readable alternatives with Jyutping and quick copying.

### Brand Personality

Native, practical, and clear. The experience should feel confidently Hong Kong without becoming playful, theatrical, or themed.

### Aesthetic Direction

A refined, contemporary utility with generous spacing, strong Cantonese typography, and restrained local character. Prefer a light interface and functional visual details over decoration.

### Design Principles

- Prioritize the Cantonese translations and Jyutping over decorative elements.
- Make daily actions such as entering, pasting, and copying text immediate and obvious.
- Use natural, concise language without stereotypes or exaggerated local slang.
- Keep spacing generous and the responsive layout comfortable on phones.
- Make feedback, focus states, and controls accessible and unambiguous.
